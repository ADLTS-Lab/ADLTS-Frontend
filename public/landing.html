How It Works

Six Steps.
Zero Gaps.

Every driving test follows a state-machine lifecycle. Each transition is governed, logged, and reversible only through explicit workflows.

🪪
Identity
OTP-verified registration, invitation-based staff onboarding, JWT sessions.
📋
Booking
Candidate submits booking. Institutes verify readiness before scheduling.
🗓
Schedule
Admin assigns time slot with capacity controls. Reschedule rules enforced.
💳
Payment
Chapa checkout with audit trail. 3× retry max. Webhook verification.
🎥
Testing
Session recorded. Score & metadata stored. MJPEG playback available.
📄
Outcome
Report generated. Appeals resolved. PDF archived for compliance.

Accountability

Trust Built In,
Not Bolted On.

Defensible by Design
ADLTS is not just convenient — it's credible. Every decision in the system is traceable to evidence: a recording, a score, a resolution. Regulators and transport authorities can audit any test, any time, with full chain-of-custody data.
🔒
Deterministic Scoring
AI assists with narrative, never with scoring. All pass/fail decisions are algorithmic and reproducible.
📹
Frame-Level Evidence
Every session records frame artifacts. Presigned URLs grant authorized access without exposing storage buckets.
⚖️
Expert-Resolved Appeals
Appeals trigger an expert review with written resolution. If accepted, test result status updates automatically.
🗂
Cached Reports
Reports are deterministic and disk-cached. The same inputs always produce the same output — forever reproducible.
